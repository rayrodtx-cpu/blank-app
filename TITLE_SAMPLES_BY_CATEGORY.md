## How to Use These Titles

### For SEO Pages:
1. Create individual landing pages for high-value searches (Alamo Heights, Stone Oak, etc.)
2. Create category pages for features (pools, 2 story, etc.)
3. Create combination pages for specific searches (3 bed 2 bath with pools in Alamo Heights)

### For URL Structure:
The CSV file includes URL slugs like:
- `homes-with-pools-in-alamo-heights`
- `2-story-homes-in-stone-oak`
- `3-bedroom-2-bathroom-homes-in-alamo-heights`

### For Meta Tags:
Use titles for:
- Page titles: "Homes with Pools in Alamo Heights | Your Realty"
- H1 headers: "Homes with Pools in Alamo Heights"
- Meta descriptions: "Find homes with pools in Alamo Heights, San Antonio. Browse luxury properties..."
