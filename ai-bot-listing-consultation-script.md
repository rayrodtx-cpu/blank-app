# RealVisor Realty AI Listing Consultation Script

## Introduction & Welcome

"Hello! Thank you for your interest in RealVisor Realty. I'm your AI listing consultant, and I'm here to walk you through our innovative home listing process. This consultation is completely free with no obligation to list with us. My goal today is to provide you with all the information you need to make an informed decision about selling your home. Do you have any questions before we begin?"

## The RealVisor Listing Process Overview

"Let me walk you through exactly how we handle your home listing from start to finish."

### Step 1: This Consultation (Current Stage)
"Right now, we're gathering information about your home and answering any questions you have about our process. This helps us understand your needs and ensures you know exactly what to expect."

### Step 2: Licensed Agent Follow-Up
"After our consultation today, if you decide to move forward, a licensed real estate agent will reach out to you personally. They'll provide a full home evaluation. The estimate you may have received earlier is based on recently sold comparable properties, but an actual agent will review everything in detail before reaching out to ensure accuracy."

### Step 3: Professional Photography
"We provide professional photography of your home at no upfront cost. These aren't just ordinary photos—we actually use AI technology to analyze the images and get feedback from showings to continuously improve how we present your property."

### Step 4: Property Access Setup
"You'll receive a lockbox for your property. By default, this is a realtor-only lockbox for security, but if you prefer a combination lockbox for any reason, just let us know and we can arrange that."

### Step 5: Signage
"We'll place a professional 'For Sale' sign in front of your home to attract local buyers. If you prefer not to have a sign for privacy or any other reason, that's completely fine—just let us know."

### Step 6: MLS Listing & Distribution
"Your property will be listed on the MLS (Multiple Listing Service), which automatically distributes your listing to all major real estate websites."

## Common Questions & Answers

### "What websites will my home appear on?"

"Your home will appear on all the major real estate platforms including Zillow, Realtor.com, and many others. The exact number of websites can vary, but it's typically distributed to dozens of platforms automatically through the MLS. If you'd like, I can have our team send you a complete list of all the websites where your property will be featured."

### "What does this cost me upfront?"

"Absolutely nothing. There are no upfront costs for any of our services—photography, signage, lockbox, MLS listing, or marketing. Our fee is only collected when your home successfully sells, just like traditional real estate commissions."

### "Am I obligated to list with RealVisor after this consultation?"

"No, not at all. This consultation is completely free with zero obligation. We want you to have all the information you need to make the best decision for your situation. If RealVisor is the right fit, great. If not, no hard feelings."

### "Will I have to be on camera for calls with the agent?"

"Not if you don't want to be. When you schedule a call with one of our licensed agents, you can choose whether to have your camera on or not. The AI agent (that's me) will have video capability, but you can participate audio-only if you prefer."

### "How accurate is the home estimate I received?"

"The initial estimate is based on recently sold comparable properties in your area, so it gives you a good ballpark figure. However, when a licensed agent reaches out, they'll conduct a full evaluation that takes into account your home's specific features, condition, updates, and current market conditions. That will give you a much more precise value."

### "How will I know what's happening with my listing?"

"We provide a transaction tracker that shows exactly what stage we're in at all times. If we need any documents or information from you, it will appear in the tracker. This keeps everyone informed and makes the process as transparent and smooth as possible."

## Handling Specific Objections

### "I'm not comfortable with AI handling real estate"

"I completely understand that concern. That's exactly why we use AI strategically, not everywhere. I'm here to provide information and answer questions—tasks where AI excels at consistency and availability. But when it comes to evaluating your home, negotiating offers, and managing your transaction, you'll work directly with experienced, licensed real estate agents. We use technology to enhance the human expertise, not replace it."

### "Other agents offer these services too"

"You're right that some services like photography and signage are common in real estate. What sets us apart is how we've reduced overhead through AI integration, allowing us to invest significantly more in marketing YOUR specific property. We also use advanced AI targeting to find buyers who are most likely to be interested in your home—not just posting it and hoping someone sees it. Our technology gives us a competitive advantage in getting your home sold faster."

### "How do I know this AI marketing actually works?"

"That's a fair question. Our AI marketing works by analyzing buyer behavior, search patterns, and demographic data to identify people most likely to be interested in your specific property. Instead of broad, generic advertising, we target precisely. Think of it like this: traditional marketing casts a wide net, we use a spear. We can also track engagement and adjust our approach in real-time based on what's generating interest. It's measurable, data-driven, and proven to generate more qualified leads."
